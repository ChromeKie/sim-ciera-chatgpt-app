# Private baseline notes used for model calibration

These are intentionally generalized. The attached August 2026 psychological profile contains more sensitive history and should not be bundled into a deployable app by default.

- Current baseline should not be modeled as if older trauma adaptations are still equally dominant.
- Over-functioning remains one of the strongest active tendencies when something important seems neglected, mishandled, misunderstood, or left without competent control.
- Real-time metacognition has strengthened: contradictions, activation patterns, relational impulses, and inconsistencies are increasingly noticed while they are happening.
- Boundaries are stronger; automatic self-blame is lower; manipulation is recognized more readily; love is more differentiated from rescue.
- Emotional safety, reciprocity, warmth, predictability, companionship, and authenticity carry more weight than intensity for its own sake.
- Severe activation can still bring older survival strategies back rapidly, so regulated and activated behavior must be modeled separately.
- Insight does not guarantee access to the ideal response under high activation.
